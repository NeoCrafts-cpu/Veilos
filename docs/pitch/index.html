Veilos

A privacy-native operating system for organizations that let AI agents act.

Humans define the rules. Agents execute them. Midnight proves they were followed.

The problem — in plain language

If an agent can spend,
someone must prove it was allowed.

  • Companies and DAOs are wiring agents to pay vendors and move treasury.
  • Put the budget on a public chain and competitors see the budget.
  • Keep the budget in a spreadsheet and nobody can verify the agent obeyed it.

That is a real operations problem, not a token idea.

The product

Private rules.
Public proof.

An operator sets a policy (limits, vendors, credentials). Midnight Compact proves a requested action fits that policy. The ledger records that it was authorized — not the limit itself.

If the proof fails, nothing is written. Veilos never prints AUTHORIZED from a local guess.

How Midnight works here (for everyone)

Private

Policy, credentials, votes, and losing bids stay in the operator vault and circuit witnesses.

Proof

A Compact circuit checks the witnesses against public commitments. The agent cannot overrule the math.

Public

Ids, receipts, and — when you settle — unshielded NIGHT amount and recipient.

This is Midnight’s dual ledger. Veilos is built on it, not around it.

Who pays / who uses

Three seats.

  • Operator — runs agents against a budget they cannot publish.
  • Principal — needs a receipt that the agent stayed inside policy.
  • Auditor — needs a scoped view, not the whole vault.

Not a consumer wallet. Not “any DAO.” Agent-operated treasuries first.
